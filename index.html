How I Think

The analytical process

01

Problem Understanding

Clarify the decision at stake before touching data. What changes downstream if the answer is X vs Y?

02

Data Exploration

Surface distributions, gaps, and anomalies first. Good EDA prevents bad models and reveals the real story.

03

Method Selection

Choose the simplest model that answers the question honestly. Complexity earns its place through evidence, not instinct.

04

Interpretation

Results without context are noise. I focus on what the numbers mean for the original decision, including uncertainty.

Bengaluru Real Estate Price Prediction

+
Problem

What factors explain variation in residential property prices across Bengaluru's heterogeneous real estate market?

Approach

Applied OLS regression to 185 housing listings, examining locality, floor area, and amenities while validating findings against hedonic pricing theory.

Result

The model explained approximately 70% of observed price variance, with locality and configuration contributing substantially to price differences.
