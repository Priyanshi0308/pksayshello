How I approach problems

I start by questioning the question. Before any model or query, I ask: what decision does this analysis actually enable? That framing shapes everything, which data matters, which method fits, what counts as a good answer.

What excites me

How I Think

The analytical process

01

Problem Understanding

Clarify the decision at stake before touching data. What changes downstream if the answer is X vs Y?

02

Data Exploration

Surface distributions, gaps, and anomalies first. Good EDA prevents bad models and reveals the real story.

03

Method Selection

Choose the simplest model that answers the question honestly. Complexity earns its place through evidence, not instinct.

04

Interpretation

Results without context are noise. I focus on what the numbers mean for the original decision, including uncertainty.

Selected Work

Machine Learning

CrimeCast: Forecasting Crime Categories

+
Problem

Can crime category be predicted from contextual variables to better allocate police resources?

Approach

EDA to surface class imbalances, feature engineering on temporal/spatial variables, compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost with GridSearchCV tuning.

Key Insight

Time-of-day and location type were the strongest predictors — not demographic variables. 85.7% accuracy with XGBoost.

Machine Learning

Bengaluru Housing Price Prediction

+
Problem

What drives housing prices in Bengaluru's heterogeneous real estate market?

Approach

Applied multiple regression with econometric methods — tested for multicollinearity, heteroskedasticity, and specification errors before finalising the model.

Key Insight

Locality and BHK configuration together explain over 70% of price variance — square footage alone is a weak predictor.

Research & Economics

Sustainability & Consumer Behaviour in Fashion

+
Problem

Does green fashion marketing actually change consumer purchasing behaviour, or is it performative?

Approach

Quantitative survey design + econometric analysis. Used regression to isolate the effect of green claims while controlling for price sensitivity and brand loyalty.

Key Insight

Green signalling increases stated intent but the price premium tolerance is narrower than brands assume — behavioural gap persists.
